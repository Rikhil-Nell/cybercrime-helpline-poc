# Cyber safety tips (from cybercrime.gov.in advisories)

## General hygiene
- Keep phones and computers updated; install apps only from official stores.
- Use strong passwords and two-factor authentication on email and banking.
- Never click suspicious links or attachments from unknown senders.
- Cover webcams when not in use.

## Social media
- Review privacy settings; limit who sees photos and personal details.
- Be cautious accepting friend requests from strangers.
- Know how to block and report abusive accounts.
- Video chats can be recorded — be careful with intimate conversations on camera.

## Financial safety
- Never share OTPs, PINs, or remote-access to devices with unknown callers.
- Verify customer care numbers from official bank websites, not search ads.
- Be skeptical of high-return investment schemes on Telegram or WhatsApp.
- Browse banking sites only on your own trusted device and network.

## Parents and teens
- Talk to children about grooming, bullying, and stalking online.
- Notice behaviour changes if a child becomes secretive about online activity.
- Report CSEAM or RGR content to the platform and cybercrime.gov.in.

## Organizations
- Maintain clear policies on obscene or illegal content in the workplace.
- Report internal incidents through proper HR and legal channels plus the portal when appropriate.
