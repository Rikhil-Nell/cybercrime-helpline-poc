# When to use state nodal / grievance contacts

## Page
https://cybercrime.gov.in/Webform/Crime_NodalGrivanceList.aspx (Contact Us)

## Not for first-time reporting
- **Money just lost** → call **1930** first, then file on portal
- **New incident** → file complaint on portal (Financial Fraud / Other Cyber Crime / Women-Child track)
- State contacts are for **follow-up and escalation**, not replacing the national portal

## When to give state contacts
1. Caller already filed with **Register & Track** and status has not moved
2. Portal response was inappropriate or insufficient (per official note on Contact Us page)
3. Caller asks for a **local cyber cell phone or email**
4. Caller wants to **download the PDF** to share with family or keep offline

## What the page offers
- Searchable table: filter by state, nodal officer name, or grievance officer name
- **Download PDF** — full list export
- Two roles per state:
  - **Nodal Cyber Cell Officer** — state cyber operations
  - **Grievance Officer** — escalation contact

## Email format on site
`[at]` = @, `[dot]` = ., `[hyphen]` = - — decode when reading aloud or texting details to caller
