# Filing a complaint on cybercrime.gov.in

## Portal purpose
The National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (https://www.cybercrime.gov.in) lets victims report cyber crimes online. It covers financial fraud, social media crimes, ransomware, hacking, cryptocurrency crimes, and a dedicated women/child track.

## Two main tracks
1. **Report Crime Against Women/Child** — CSEAM, RGR content, sextortion involving minors, related harassment.
2. **Report Other Cyber Crime** — financial fraud, impersonation, ransomware, hacking, crypto, mobile crimes, trafficking, gambling, and other categories.

## Registration basics
- Valid **Indian mobile number** required.
- **OTP verification** on the registered mobile.
- Select the correct **category and sub-category** — this routes the complaint.
- Provide accurate details: dates, platforms, amounts, suspect handles where known.

## Evidence uploads
Upload supporting material when available:
- Screenshots of chats, profiles, transaction records
- Email headers, URLs, phone numbers
- Audio/video files if relevant and safe to share

## After filing
- Save the **acknowledgment / complaint reference** shown on the portal.
- Use portal tracking features to follow status when available.
- Keep copies of everything you submitted.

## False complaints
Knowingly filing false information can have legal consequences. Report truthfully to the best of your knowledge.

## Cannot upload evidence?
Some complaint flows allow filing with minimal details first — check current portal options. Still report as soon as possible for financial fraud (time-sensitive).
