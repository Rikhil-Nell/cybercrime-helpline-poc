# State/UT Nodal & Grievance Officers — Contact Us

**Portal page:** https://cybercrime.gov.in/Webform/Crime_NodalGrivanceList.aspx  
**Also reachable from:** top menu → Contact Us, or footer → Contact Us

## What this page is
Official list of **State/UT Nodal Cyber Cell Officers** and **Grievance Officers** for every state and union territory. Use when a caller needs a **local escalation contact** after filing on the portal.

## When the agent should mention it
- Caller filed via **Register & Track** and got **no response or an unsatisfactory response**
- Caller asks "who do I contact in my state?"
- Caller wants a **phone number or email** beyond 1930 / national portal
- Financial fraud: page banner reminds — report online financial fraud at **1930** first; state contacts are for follow-up/escalation

## How to use the page (guide the caller)
1. Open Contact Us on cybercrime.gov.in
2. **Search** by state name, nodal officer name, or grievance officer name
3. Or scroll the table for their state/UT
4. **Download PDF** button exports the full list for offline reference

## Reading email addresses on the page
Emails are obfuscated to reduce spam scraping:
- `[at]` → `@`
- `[dot]` → `.`
- `[hyphen]` → `-`

Example: `cybercrimes1930[at]cid[dot]appolice[dot]gov[dot]in` → cybercrimes1930@cid.appolice.gov.in

## Two columns per state
| Role | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **Nodal Cyber Cell Officer** | State cyber crime cell lead — operational contact for cyber complaints |
| **Grievance Officer** | Escalation if portal complaint handling was not appropriate |
