# Official resources — National Cyber Crime Portal

## Helplines
- **1930** — National cyber financial fraud helpline (urgent money-loss cases)
- **112** — Emergency services (immediate physical danger)

## Primary portal
- **https://www.cybercrime.gov.in** — file and track cyber crime complaints

## Portal resource pages (Ministry of Home Affairs / I4C)
- **FAQ** — https://www.cybercrime.gov.in/Webform/FAQ.aspx
- **Citizen manuals** — https://www.cybercrime.gov.in/Webform/Citizen_Manual.aspx
  - General cyber crime reporting manual
  - RGR complaints manual
  - Financial cyber frauds reporting and management system manual
- **Cyber awareness booklets** — https://cybercrime.gov.in/webform/cyberaware.aspx
- **Online safety tips & student handbooks** — https://cybercrime.gov.in/webform/Crime_OnlineSafetyTips.aspx

## Handbook references (download from portal)
- Handbook for Tackling Cyber Crimes
- Handbook for Students on Cyber Safety (English and Hindi)

## State/UT contacts (escalation)
- **Contact Us / Nodal & Grievance list** — https://cybercrime.gov.in/Webform/Crime_NodalGrivanceList.aspx
  - Searchable table of state cyber cell nodal officers and grievance officers
  - **Download PDF** button for offline copy
  - Use when caller filed via Register & Track and needs local escalation

## When to use which channel
- **Money just lost** → 1930 first, then portal complaint
- **Sexual content involving minors or women/child track** → portal women/child reporting track
- **General cybercrime** → portal other cybercrime track
- **No portal response after filing** → state nodal or grievance officer (Contact Us page)
- **Prevention questions** → safety tips and awareness booklets on portal
