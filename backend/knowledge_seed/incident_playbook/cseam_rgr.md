# Immediate steps — CSEAM / RGR content (women & child track)

## Portal track
Use **Report Crime Against Women/Child** on https://www.cybercrime.gov.in — not the general cybercrime form.

## Categories on portal
- Child Sexual Exploitative and Abuse Material (CSEAM)
- Rape/Gang Rape (RGR) content circulated online
- Related cyber bullying, stalking, or harassment against women and children

## Evidence
- URLs where content appears, account handles, platform names
- Screenshots with timestamps (handle carefully — do not re-share illegal content)
- Any messages threatening to publish material

## Immediate safety
- Report the content to the **platform** (report button) in parallel with the portal complaint.
- Secure the victim's accounts; change passwords; review privacy settings.

## Legal note for callers
Publication, collection, and distribution of CSEAM is illegal under the Information Technology Act (Sections 67, 67A, 67B).
