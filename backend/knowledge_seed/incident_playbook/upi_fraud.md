# Immediate steps — UPI / payment-app fraud

## Stop the bleeding
- Open your UPI app → check **transaction history** → note UPI reference numbers.
- Call **1930** immediately with transaction details.
- In your UPI app or bank app, look for **report fraud** or **dispute** options where available.
- If you authorized a collect request by mistake, report it as unauthorized.

## Common UPI scam patterns
- Fake customer care numbers in Google search results
- "Wrong transfer" refund requests asking you to send money back
- QR code swaps at shops
- Remote screen-sharing to "help" with KYC
- Fake investment returns asking for repeated UPI payments

## Preserve proof
- Screenshot the transaction with UPI ID of recipient
- Save chat screenshots with the person who induced the payment
- Note the phone number linked to the UPI ID if visible

## Portal filing
Report on https://www.cybercrime.gov.in under online financial fraud. Upload transaction screenshots and chat evidence.
