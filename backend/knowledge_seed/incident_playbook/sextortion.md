# Immediate steps — sextortion / intimate image blackmail

## Do not pay
- Paying rarely stops harassment and often leads to repeated demands.
- Do not send more images or videos.

## Secure accounts
- Change passwords on social media, email, and messaging apps.
- Review logged-in devices and remove unknown sessions.
- Set accounts to private; tighten friend lists.

## Preserve evidence
- Save chats, profile URLs, payment demands — screenshots with timestamps.
- Do not delete the blackmailer's account until evidence is saved.

## Report
- **Women/child sexual content or minors involved** → use the **Report Crime Against Women/Child** track on cybercrime.gov.in (CSEAM / RGR categories as applicable).
- **Adult sextortion** → **Report Other Cyber Crime** with available sub-categories (social media / online fraud as applicable).

## Support
- If you feel unsafe, reach out to someone you trust locally.
- For child-related material, report urgently — distribution is a serious offence under IT Act sections.
