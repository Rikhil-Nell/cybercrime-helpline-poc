# Immediate steps — digital arrest scam

## Recognize the scam
- Real police **do not** arrest people over WhatsApp, Skype, or Zoom.
- No agency demands money over video call to "clear your name" or avoid arrest.
- Threats about "CBI/NCB/customs" packages, money laundering, or Aadhaar misuse on video are a **known fraud pattern**.

## What to do right now
1. **Hang up** the video or voice call. Block the number.
2. **Do not transfer money** — not even "verification" or "legal fees".
3. If you already paid, follow financial fraud steps: **1930**, bank, then cybercrime.gov.in.
4. Tell family you are safe — scammers sometimes contact relatives to extort.

## Preserve evidence
- Screenshots of video call participants and chat
- Payment receipts if money was sent
- Phone numbers and Telegram IDs used

## Portal category
File under **Report Other Cyber Crime** → impersonation / online financial fraud as applicable.
