# Immediate steps — online financial fraud

## First hour (urgent)
1. **Call 1930** — National cyber financial fraud helpline. Report the fraudulent transaction details while memory is fresh.
2. **Call your bank or wallet provider** on their official customer care number (from your passbook, card, or official app — not a number the scammer gave you). Ask to block further debits and report fraud.
3. **Do not send more money** — scammers often ask for "processing fees", "tax", or "refund verification". That is part of the fraud.
4. **Preserve evidence** — screenshots of chats, UPI references, transaction IDs, caller numbers, email headers, and any links. Do not delete messages yet.

## Within 24 hours
5. **File a complaint** on the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal: https://www.cybercrime.gov.in — choose **Report Other Cyber Crime** → **Online Financial Fraud** (or the closest matching sub-category).
6. **Note your acknowledgment number** after filing. You can track status on the portal.
7. If you shared OTPs or passwords, **change passwords** and enable two-factor authentication on email and banking apps.

## Evidence to collect
- Date and time of each transaction
- Amount, UPI ID / account number money was sent to
- App used (PhonePe, GPay, Paytm, net banking, etc.)
- Scammer phone numbers, Telegram/WhatsApp IDs, website URLs
- How contact started (call, SMS, social media ad, job offer)

## What not to do
- Do not confront the scammer expecting a refund
- Do not install remote-access apps (AnyDesk, TeamViewer) if someone unknown asks
- Do not share Aadhaar, PAN, or bank login credentials
