# Reassurance — financial cyber fraud (India)

## You are not "finished"
Many callers believe money lost to online fraud is gone forever. That is not always true. **Acting quickly matters.**

## Why speed helps
- The **1930 helpline** connects to the cyber financial fraud reporting and management process (CFCFRMS). Banks and payment networks can sometimes **hold or reverse** fraudulent transfers if reported early.
- Recovery is **not guaranteed** — depends on timing, whether funds moved onward, and institution policies. But **not reporting removes the chance entirely**.

## Emotional framing (for the agent)
- Scammers are skilled; shame belongs with the criminal, not the victim.
- Taking the next step — 1930, bank, portal — is the responsible action.
- Do **not** cite specific past cases or promise "you will get everything back."

## Realistic hope
- Partial recovery happens in some time-sensitive cases.
- Even without recovery, a formal complaint creates an official record for investigation and future action.
- Stopping further loss is already a win.
