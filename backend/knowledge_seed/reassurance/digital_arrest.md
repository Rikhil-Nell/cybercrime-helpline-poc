# Reassurance — digital arrest scams

## You are not under real arrest
If someone on a video call claimed to be police, CBI, customs, or NCB and demanded money — **that is a scam**. Real law enforcement does not conduct arrests or collect fines over WhatsApp or Zoom.

## You did the right thing by questioning it
Many educated professionals have fallen for digital arrest fraud. The scripts are convincing and use fear.

## If you paid
Money can sometimes be traced if reported quickly via **1930** and your bank. Follow financial fraud playbook steps without shame.

## Going forward
- Government agencies send official written communication through established channels, not payment links on video calls.
- When in doubt, disconnect and call the organization's **publicly listed** number — not one the caller provides.
