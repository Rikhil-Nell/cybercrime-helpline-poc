# 1930 Helpline Procedures

## Reporting a financial fraud
If money was debited due to fraud, the caller should report within the golden hour. Call 1930 immediately and then file a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in. The sooner the report, the higher the chance of freezing the transaction.

## Freezing a transaction
Once a complaint is filed, the bank nodal officer is alerted through the Citizen Financial Cyber Fraud Reporting system. The bank can place a lien on the beneficiary account to stop further withdrawal.

## UPI fraud steps
For UPI fraud, note the UPI transaction ID, the amount, and the beneficiary VPA. Report these details to the 1930 operator. Do not share OTP or PIN with anyone claiming to help.
